## TASK 1. Kemenpar

How the pages work: each statistic page is `app/(home)/[articles]/page` (Next.js). The page's RSC payload (inline `self.__next_f`) carries a `config` object with `module: "statistics/posts"` and `filterData.category_id`. The generic fetcher in chunk 8174 calls `https://api.kemenpar.go.id/api/v1/statistics/posts?pageSize&query&filterData={"category_id":N}&order_by=published_at&order_dir=desc&pageIndex`. No headless browser was needed. Category ids: jasa-perjalanan 25, devisa 27, wisnas 22, wisman 21, satelit 26, PES 30, nesparnas 35, kunjungan-wisman 29, direktori -1 (all; 277 posts). Posts carry an HTML description with a download link or a `files[]` array (storage URLs).

**Answer: no by-country travel balance exists on Kemenpar.**
* `/statistik-jasa-perjalanan` is NOT balance-of-payments travel services. It holds 3 posts (2007-2011, travel business statistics): "Rekapitulasi Usaha Jasa Perjalanan Wisata Berskala Menengah dan Besar", "Perkembangan Biro Perjalanan Wisata (BPW) Berskala Menengah dan Besar Menurut Provinsi", "Perkembangan Agen Perjalanan Wisata (APW) Berskala Menengah dan Besar Menurut Provinsi". Business counts of travel agencies, no money flows by partner country.
* `/statistik-devisa-pariwisata` holds 7 posts titled "Perkembangan Jumlah Devisa Sektor Pariwisata Tahun 2015-2024" and "... Triwulan I/II/III/IV Tahun 2025/2026". One-page PDFs, TOTALS only (source: Bank Indonesia BoP, "diolah kembali" by Biro Data dan Sistem Informasi). Credit side only, no country, no debit. Examples: 2023 USD 14,000.91m, 2024 16,703.30m, 2025* 18,274.65m (also 2015-2024 annual USD bn in kemenpar_totals.csv).
* The nearest things (all inbound/credit side, none a balance):
  * "Devisa (Penerimaan) Wisatawan Mancanegara Menurut Negara Tempat Tinggal, 2009-2014" (Satuan juta USD; source PES): receipts BY COUNTRY, 38 countries/regions + total (2014 total USD 11,166.13m; Malaysia 1,053.89; Singapura 1,145.83; Arab Saudi 198.20; Australia 1,802.85). Rows sum to the total within 0.04. Extracted.
  * "Rata-Rata Pengeluaran Wisatawan Mancanegara per Kunjungan Menurut Negara Tempat Tinggal (US $)" 2015-2024 (2017 not published; ASPA table) and "... Menurut Paspor yang Dipegang" 2015-2025 (selected markets). Extracted. Also per-day and length-of-stay tables for 2009-2014.
  * "Neraca Jasa Perjalanan Indonesia Tahun 2010-2016" (direktori category): one page of BI totals (export 2015 10,761; import -7,292; net 3,469, USD m). Extracted to kemenpar_totals.csv.
* Outbound by destination country: none. Nesparnas 2015 Buku 1 (PDF p.32-33, printed pp.24-25, section 2.3.2): "apabila ingin dilihat negara tujuan mereka di luar negeri masih belum bisa terpenuhi dari kartu kedatangan dan keberangkatan (A/D Card) untuk Warga Negara Indonesia (WNI), karena dalam kartu tersebut tidak ditanyakan negara tujuan yang akan dikunjungi" and outbound spending comes from an Outbound Survey that is "tidak dilaksanakan setiap tahun, dan survei terakhir dilaksanakan pada tahun 2013". Nesparnas 2014 Buku 1 p.66 says only that wisnas go mostly to Malaysia and Singapore (qualitative). The "Perkembangan Wisnas (OUTBOUND)" sheet (`rekap_wisnas.pdf`) gives totals only: 2011 6,750,416 trips, USD 934.50/person, 7.67 days, USD 6,308.26m; 2012 7,453,633 (estimate; no outbound survey that year), USD 926.20, USD 6,903.55m; 2013 8,024,876, USD 912.31, USD 7,321.21m; 2014-2016 counts only (7,899,070; 7,908,534; 6,677,918 Jan-Oct).

## TASK 3. Hajj and umrah (`hajj_umrah.csv`; source_type column)

Source quality is mixed and stated per row: `official` (BPS WebAPI, which republishes Kemenag), `official_via_news` (a Kemenag/DPR/Panja statement quoted by Antara or detik; the primary document was not retrieved), `news` (detik compilation), `industry`.
* Pilgrims: BPS var 178 (regular hajj via Kemenag, national total): 2013 154,546; 2014 154,467; **2015 154,455; 2016 154,441** (series ends 2016; earlier 2007-2012 included). Quotas (regular+special; Kemenag figures compiled by detik): 2015 168,800 (155,200+13,600); 2016 168,800; 2017 221,000 (204,000+17,000); 2018 221,000; 2019 231,000 (214,000+17,000); 2020-2021 cancelled (0); 2022 100,051 (92,825+7,226); 2023 229,000 (base 221,000 + 8,000 extra; **realised 228,093 = 99.6%**, Antara quoting Dirjen PHU); 2024 241,000 (213,320+27,680); 2025 221,000 (203,320+17,680). Realised departures for 2017-2019, 2022, 2024, 2025 were not found; quota is the proxy (2015-16 realised regular was 99.5% of regular quota). Treat news compilations with care: detik d-8594467 wrongly lists 2023 as 221,000.
* Cost per pilgrim (IDR, regular): 2015 BPIH USD 2,717 (Perpres 64/2015); 2016 Rp34.64m (USD 2,585); from 2017 the BPIH = Bipih (paid) + nilai manfaat (BPKH subsidy): 2017 61.79 = 34.89 + 26.90; 2018 68.96 = 35.24 + 33.72; 2019 69.16 = 35.24 + 33.92; 2022 97.79 = 38.89 + 57.91; 2023 90.05 = 49.81 + 40.23; 2024 93.41 = 56.05 + 37.36 (exact 93,410,286 / 56,046,171.60); **2025 89,410,258.79 = 55,431,750.78 + 33,978,508.01** (Antara quoting Panja chair; matches the brief and Keppres 6/2025); 2026 87,409,365.45 = 54,193,806.58 + 33,215,558.87. Bipih by embarkasi 2025 (Keppres 6/2025) for 4 embarkasi are included (full list in the saved page).
* Component split: the DPR document (berkas.dpr.go.id/...9c99c60e...pdf) returned **HTTP 403 (Akamai Access Denied)**, also with a Referer; not bypassed. Found instead: (a) 2025 Kemenag **proposal** (30 Dec 2024; Bipih 65,372,779): flight Rp34,386,390.68, Makkah accommodation 15,232,011.90, Madinah 4,454,403.48, living cost 3,200,002.50, masyair package (part) 8,099,970.94; (b) final 2025 flight Rp33,100,000 (same in 2026); (c) 2026 government proposal for Bipih (Rp54,924,000): flight 33,100,000, Makkah 14,652,000, Madinah 3,872,000, living cost 3,300,000; (d) 2027 final BPIH Rp98,466,351.35 = domestic 5,643,945.28 + costs in Saudi Arabia 54,317,360.07 + flight 38,505,046. The full final 2025 BPIH split (including the nilai-manfaat-funded masyair, catering, etc.) was not found. Note the flight line is the round trip from the embarkasi, and which airline is paid (Garuda/Saudia) matters for how much counts as a payment to Saudi residents.
* Umrah: only one count found: about 1.6 million Indonesians in 2025 (Siskopatuh, quoted by Antara in a BSI article). No 2015-2024 series (gap). Reference cost: KMA 1021/2023 "referensi biaya" about Rp23 juta (the brief's KMA 777/2020 Rp26 juta was not retrieved and appears superseded). Industry/news package prices 2025: regular Rp24-29m, premium Rp28-39m, special Rp32-100m; flight to Saudi about Rp8m one way. Saudi side: 18.03m foreign umrah visitors and 1.5m hajj pilgrims in 2025 (PEP annual report via detik); no Indonesia split.
